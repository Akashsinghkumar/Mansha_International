LINK JACQUARD

LINK JACQUARD Computer Electronic Jacquard Circular knitting machines
LINK JACQUARD Computer Electronic Jacquard Circular knitting machines equipped with advanced 3-way computerized needle selection and synchronized multi-yarn guides for complex relief and pattern knits.
  • 1. Electronic System: Machine 3 way Chuangda Electronic jacquard computer system with microsecond actuator response.
  • 2. Synchronized Feeder: Special Yarn guide possible for 3 yarn feeding at the same time.
  • 3. Advanced CAD Telemetry: High-definition touchscreen monitor with USB pattern loading and automatic fault detection.

SINGLE JACQUARD

Single Jersey Electronic Computerized Jacquard Circular Machine
High-speed electronic jacquard system for high-resolution single jersey patterned knits, Lycra fashion activewear, and mattress ticking fabrics.
  • 3-Way needle selection (knit, tuck, miss) on cylinder for boundless geometric pattern capabilities.
  • Ultra-responsive piezoelectric ceramic actuators with minimal heat generation.
  • Central stitch adjustment with micro-meter calibrations for rapid fabric density (GSM) tuning.

DOUBLE JACQUARD

Dual-Selection Dial & Cylinder Computerized Double Jersey Jacquard Machine
Engineered for high-end double face jacquards, multi-color relief patterns, heavy winter apparel knits, and quilted home textile fabrics.
  • Independent computerized selection on both cylinder and dial needle beds.
  • Reversible double-face cloth production with crisp pattern resolution on both sides.
  • Rigid industrial cast frame absorbs torsional stress during continuous high RPM operation.

SINGLE TERRY JACQUARD

Plush, Towel & Velvet Electronic Jacquard Circular Knitting Machine
Combines plush loop formation with computerized jacquard needle selection for sculpted velvet, embossed hotel bath towels, and patterned polar fleece.
  • Sculpted plush knits: Creates multi-height terry loops and flat jacquard zones in the same fabric.
  • Zero yarn shear with hardened high-wear sinker cams protecting delicate cotton and micro-yarns.
  • Central stitch adjustment dial and precision ball bearing system ensures energy savings.

DOUBLE JACQUARD RIB TRANSFER

Advanced Rib & Interlock Transfer Jacquard Machine
High-precision circular knitting machine with needle transfer capabilities between cylinder and dial for eyelet jacquard, pointelle, drop-stitch patterns, and open-work knitwear.
  • Automated cylinder-to-dial stitch transfer mechanism without dropped stitches.
  • Produces delicate lace-effect eyelets, open-mesh pointelle, and fashion rib knitwear.
  • Oil-bathed gear structure ensures whisper-quiet running and long service life.
