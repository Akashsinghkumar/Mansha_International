Advanced Circular Knitting Machinery & Servicing

Advanced circular knitting machines engineered for high-speed fabric production, delivering exceptional precision, consistent quality, & reliable performance.

We supply Single Jersey, Double Jersey, Interlock, Rib, Fleece, and Jacquard circular knitting machines. Our services include machine installation, inverter tuning, cylinder height adjustment, and oil lubrication system inspection.

Service Highlights:

  • Single Jersey & Double Jersey Machine Supply & Commissioning
  • High-speed RPM rotation stability with zero vibration frame design
  • In-house cylinder gauge conversion and needle bed alignment
  • Auto-stop motion sensors and electronic fabric roll take-up
  • Emergency troubleshooting & routine preventive maintenance plans
