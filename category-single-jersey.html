Single Jersey Circular Knitting Machine (6-Track Expert Series)

High-Speed 6-Track Single Jersey Circular Knitting Machine
Our expert Single Jersey Circular Knitting Machine features Central stitch adjustment, easy to operate and fast setting of the machine. The machine is ball bearing driven, so the machine is light and ensures smooth driving, less noise, and saves your energy expense.
  • Arranges 6-track cylinder cams (knit, tuck, miss) with expanded pattern versatility for plain jersey, pique, twill, and mesh designs.
  • Precision ball bearing drive ensures low noise, minimal mechanical wear, and reduced energy costs.
  • Positive storage feeders deliver uniform yarn tension with zero horizontal bar marks.

Terry Circular Knitting Machine

Face Terry Circular Knitting Machine for Polar Fleece & Reverse Terry for Velvet
Terry machines are engineered with Central Stitch adjustment and a precision Ball Bearing system to deliver plush, upright loop structures without yarn shear.
  • Face Terry for fluffy polar fleece thermal outdoor apparel.
  • Reverse Terry for premium dense velvet, velour sportswear, and bath towels.
  • Low-friction ball-bearing drive ensures light, smooth rotation and reduces electric power costs.

High Speed Three Thread Fleece Circular Knitting Machines

Single Jersey Three Thread Fleece Circular Knitting Machine
Mansha Three Thread Fleece Circular Knitting Machine: Four track cams machine equipped with Groz-Beckert needles, central stitch adjustment, and a precision ball bearing system for easy operation, smooth driving, and maximum heavy fleece stability.
  • Four-track cam box knits face, ground, and heavy tie-in inlay fleece loops simultaneously.
  • High loop density engineered for brushing, sheared hoodies, sweatpants, and winter jackets.
  • Central stitch adjustment enables rapid fabric weight (GSM) tuning without downtime.
