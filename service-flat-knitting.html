High-Performance Flat Knitting Machinery & Support

Advanced computerized flat knitting machines engineered for manufacturing premium-quality sweaters, cardigans, pullovers, collars, cuffs, and shaped panels with superior efficiency & precision.

At Mansha International, we supply state-of-the-art single, dual, and triple system flat knitting machines built to handle intricate Jacquard designs, Intarsia color blocks, cables, pointelle, and full-fashion shaping. Our experienced technical team ensures proper machine programming, tension calibration, CAD pattern installation, and operator training for maximum yield.

Service Features & Specifications:

  • Computerized Single, Dual & Triple System Flat Knitting Machinery Supply
  • Comprehensive support for 3G, 5G, 7G, 12G, 14G, 16G & multi-gauge setups
  • Dynamic stepper stitch adjustment, servo racking calibration & zero-defect tuning
  • Pattern CAD software programming guidance and on-site factory operator training
  • Immediate availability of genuine latch needles, sinkers, yarn feeders, and spare parts
