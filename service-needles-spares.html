Genuine Components

High-Quality Knitting Needles & Essential Spare Parts

We provide high-quality knitting needles and essential spare parts designed to maintain the smooth and efficient operation of knitting machines. Our components are built for durability, precision, and reliable performance.

Using genuine needles and precision sinkers prevents yarn breakage, reduces fabric drop stitches, and minimizes friction wear on cylinder walls. We maintain ready inventory for all major machine brands from Germany, Japan, China, and Korea.

Spare Parts Inventory & Range:

  • High-strength Latch Needles & Transfer Needles for Circular & Glove Machines
  • Precision-milled Sinkers, Sinker Jacks, and Cam Blocks
  • Electronic positive storage feeders (MPF) and yarn tensioners
  • Oil spray lubricators, drive belts, and needle detectors
  • Same-day dispatch and express shipping across India
