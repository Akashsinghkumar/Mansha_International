Answers to Popular Enquiries

Find answers to the most common questions about our textile machinery, spare parts, installation, delivery, and technical support.

Which textile machines do you supply?

We supply Circular Knitting Machines (Single Jersey, Double Jersey, Interlock, Rib, Fleece), Cap & Gloves Knitting Machines, Second-Hand Textile Machines, Needles, Sinkers, and Machine Spare Parts.

Do you provide installation and technical support?

Yes. Our experienced technical team provides on-site installation guidance, machine setup assistance, tension calibration, routine maintenance support, and troubleshooting services across India.

How can I request a quotation?

Simply fill out the enquiry form on our website, call us directly at +91 8800335090, or send us an email at info@manshainternational.in with your required specifications. Our team will respond with the best quotation as soon as possible.

Do you supply second-hand and refurbished knitting machines?

Yes, we offer thoroughly inspected and refurbished second-hand knitting machines from top global brands, ensuring excellent operational condition at a cost-effective price point.

Where is Mansha International located?

Our main head office and stock warehouse are located at B 24-2540 Jagdambe Complex Sunder Nagar, Ludhiana - 141007, Punjab, India. We ship machinery and spare parts across India and internationally.

What yarn and machine gauges do your machines support?

Our circular and specialty knitting machines support a wide variety of gauges ranging from coarse gauges (3G, 5G, 7G) for heavy knitwear up to fine gauges (14G, 20G, 24G, 28G, 32G) for lightweight T-shirt jersey fabrics.
