ABOUT US

Textile with Technology...

At Mansha International, we believe a knitting machine is more than an investment — it's a promise to your customers, your production line, and your growth. Founded on decades of hands-on expertise and a genuine passion for innovation, we exist for one purpose: to help you turn ambition into finished fabric, reliably and profitably.

Established in 2018 and headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, we specialize in high-efficiency Circular Knitting Machines, Cap & Gloves Knitting Machines, Second-Hand Equipment, Needles, Sinkers, and Genuine Spare Parts.

Real Innovation Built on Genuine Accountability

WHY WE STARTED

After years of serving the industry from within, Manish and Deepak noticed something the market needed but wasn't getting: real innovation, delivered with real accountability. Demand was growing. Customers were ready to embrace new technology. But genuine expertise, honest guidance, and dependable support were hard to find.

So, they built the company they knew the industry deserved — one where deep technical mastery meets a customer-first mindset.

That's how Mansha International was born: not just as a machine supplier, but as a partner committed to your success.

FOUNDERS

Founded by Industry Veterans, Built for You

Mansha International (formerly Mansha Knit) is the vision of two industry veterans, Mr. Manish Singh and Mr. Deepak Kumar, whose combined decades of expertise now work directly for you.

Mr. Deepak Kumar brings nearly 20 years of deep, hands-on mastery in the knitting industry — built from the ground up, from the production floor to advanced machine commissioning. His tenure with Mayer & Cie (Batliboi India) gave him technical insight into world-class German circular knitting technology.

Together, our leadership team ensures that every machine supplied by Mansha International meets rigorous mechanical calibration, tension stability, and long-term operating durability.
